04 — Questions

The ones people actually ask.

Does checking my rate affect my credit score?

No. The rate check uses a soft search, which is visible only to you and leaves no mark on your file. A hard search happens only if you accept an offer and continue to signing — and we show that moment explicitly before it happens.

Can I repay early?

Yes, at any time, with no charge. You can overpay monthly or clear the whole balance; interest is only charged up to the day you settle.

How fast is the money?

Verification and signing completed before 4pm on a business day normally means funds arrive the next business day. Slower for some banks; the app shows your exact date once signed.

What happens if I miss a payment?

Contact us before the due date and we'll try to move the date. After 14 days a $15 missed-payment fee applies, and persistent missed payments are reported to credit bureaus and may affect your score.

What do you need from me?

A government ID, proof of income (payslips or two years of tax returns if self-employed), and your bank connection for Direct Debit. Nothing is mailed, nothing is faxed.
